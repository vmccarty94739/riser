# Timber: Help & Support

Timber is a habit tracker for iPhone: build good habits, break bad ones, and earn trophies for showing up every day.

**Need help?** Email **[riserapp.support@gmail.com](mailto:riserapp.support@gmail.com)**. We usually reply within two business days.

## Common questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. Timber works on its own and backs your habits up to a private guest account automatically. Add an email and password (Settings → Account → Create account) if you want to sign in on another phone.

**I didn't get my code email.**
Check your spam folder, then wait a minute and try again. Codes expire after one hour, and only the newest code works.

**I forgot my password.**
In Timber, go to Settings → Account → I already have an account → Forgot password?, and we'll email you a code to set a new one.

**How do I move to a new phone?**
Create an account on your old phone first (Settings → Account → Create account), then sign in with it on the new phone. Proof photos stay on the phone they were taken on.

**How do I delete my account and data?**
Settings → Account → Delete account (or Settings → Erase all data for a guest). This permanently removes your account and all of its data from our servers and your phone. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

**My reminders don't arrive.**
Make sure notifications are allowed for Timber in the iPhone Settings app, and that Daily reminders is on in Timber's Settings. Timber shows the next scheduled reminder there, so you can check it's set.

**Is my data private?**
Yes. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The coach runs on your phone, and proof photos never leave it. Read the [privacy policy](privacy/).
